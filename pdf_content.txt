Dataset: Amazon Product Reviews (McAuley et al.)
1. Mission Overview
Your team has been hired as an AI Task Force by a global brand. One of their flagship products
is in trouble. While the average star rating remains deceptively stable, sales are declining and
return rates are climbing. Management is confusedΓÇöthey see 4-star averages and assume

2. The Data Challenge (Phased Roadmap)
Day 1: The Diagnostic & The "Star-Gap"
Management relies on stars; you must rely on truth.
ΓùÅ Task: Calculate a "Sentiment Score" for reviews and compare it to the "Star Rating."
ΓùÅ Key Analysis: Identify "Silent Killers"ΓÇöreviews with high star ratings (4 or 5) but negative
text sentiment.
ΓùÅ Goal: Quantify the percentage of misleadingly positive reviews and visualize the timeline
of when sentiment began to sour.
Day 2: Root Cause Analysis (Aspects & Emotions)
To fix the product, you must know what is broken and how it makes people feel.
ΓùÅ Aspect Mining: Use Topic Modeling (e.g., LDA, BERTopic) or Keyword Extraction to

ΓùÅ Emotional Mapping: Perform Emotion Detection (Joy, Anger, Disappointment, Sadness)

ΓùÅ Goal: Create a "Heatmap" linking features to emotions (e.g., "Battery" + "Anger" vs.
"Design" + "Joy").
Day 3: The Rescue Plan & Business Pitch

ΓùÅ Prioritization Matrix: Create a 2x2 grid mapping Frequency of Complaint vs.
