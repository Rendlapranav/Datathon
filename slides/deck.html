The Diagnostic & The "Star-Gap"

The Paradox of a 4.1★ Product

Management sees a stable 4-star average and assumes health. Sales data shows climbing returns. The truth lies in the text, not the stars.

The Illusion of Stars

Stars are a biased, lagging signal. Customers often round up, avoid conflict, or churn quietly. By the time the aggregated star rating actually drops, thousands of customers are already lost.

The Star-Gap Phenomenon
Mean star gap is +0.201 — stars are artificially inflating the reality of customer satisfaction.

AI Detection Protocol

  • Input Filter: Star rating ≥ 4 (High-star mask)
  • NLP Engine: RoBERTa Sentiment Model
  • Trigger: `prob_negative` > `prob_positive`
  • Calibration: Dynamic thresholding based on confirmed 1-2★ angry reviews
  • Weighting: Verified purchases & helpful votes scaled via log function
