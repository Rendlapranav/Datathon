CORE CONCEPT

Meet the Silent Killers

420

Silent Killers detected
8.4% of all reviews

DEFINITION
A Silent Killer is a reviewer who leaves 4-5★ but whose language signals high anger or disappointment (RoBERTa prob_negative ≥ data-driven threshold). They churn without filing a support ticket, leaving no trace — except in NLP.

Detection Methodology

  • Star rating ≥ 4 (high-star mask)
  • Model predicts Negative > Positive
  • prob_negative ≥ p10 of confirmed-bad (1-2★) reviews
  • sk_weight: log(helpful_votes+1) × 1.2 if verified
  • Threshold recalibrates automatically per dataset

FIX #2 + #3 — MID-TERM

Fix Connectivity & Value Perception

Fix #2 · Connectivity (7.0% of reviews)

  • Bluetooth pairing fails on Android consistently
  • Audio drops during multi-device switching
  • Action: BT stack firmware patch + improved multi-device protocol
  • Timeline: 60 days · Engineering effort: Medium

Fix #3 · Value for Money (7.1% of reviews)

  • Customers feel features don't justify price point
  • Competitor perception gap widening since 2021
  • Action: Tutorial content series + comparison landing page
  • Timeline: 30 days · Effort: Marketing (no engineering)
💡 COMBINED IMPACT
Fixing all three issues addresses 25.5% of all reviews by volume and neutralises the two highest-anger complaint clusters.
